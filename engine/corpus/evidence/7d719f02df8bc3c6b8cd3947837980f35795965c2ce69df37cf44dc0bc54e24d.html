Cancellation Policy

Cancellation Policy

Saver

Published Fares are applicable for individual bookings only.

Minimum 1 Passenger and Maximum 9 Passengers are permitted to book in a single PNR.

Fares are subject to change without any notice. Fares may be combined with some other fare type.

The below mentioned are Standard Terms & Conditions for regular fares.
Kindly refer to the Specific Terms and conditions applicable For Sale & Offers.

Cancellation Fees:

Domestic:
Departure within 0 to 3 days- INR 3500 or 100% of the airfare charge (whichever is lower)
Departure 4 days and more- INR 3000 or 100% of the airfare charge (whichever is lower)


Change Fees:

Domestic:

Departure within 0 to 3 days- INR 3000/- + difference in Base Fare / Taxes
Departure 4 days and more- INR 2500/- + difference in Base Fare / Taxes
.
No-Show Penalty: 100% of airfare charge

Baggage Allowance : 15kgs 1 Piece Only


Akasa Air reserves the right to change the Cancellation/Change Fees from time to time without any prior intimation.


For the complete Conditions of Carriage, please click here.

Flexi

Published Fares are applicable for individual bookings only.

Minimum 1 Passenger and Maximum 9 Passengers are permitted to book in a single PNR.

Fares are subject to change without any notice. Fares may be combined with some other fare type.

The below mentioned are Standard Terms & Conditions for regular fares.
Kindly refer to the Specific Terms and conditions applicable For Sale & Offers.

Cancellation Fees:

Domestic:
Departure within 0 to 3 days- INR 3500 or 100% of the airfare charge (whichever is lower)
Departure 4 days and more- INR 500 or 100% of the airfare charge (whichever is lower)

Change Fees:


Domestic:

Departure within 0 to 3 days- INR 3000/- + difference in Base Fare / Taxes
Departure 4 days and more- INR 0/- + difference in Base Fare / Taxes
.
No-Show Penalty: 100% of airfare charge

Baggage Allowance : 15kgs 1 Piece Only

Akasa Air reserves the right to change the Cancellation/Change Fees from time to time without any prior intimation.

For the complete Conditions of Carriage, please click here.
