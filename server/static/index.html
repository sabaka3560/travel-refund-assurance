How it works, and how it scales

Six steps. Only the first is AI — everything that touches a rupee is deterministic Python.

1
Read the ticket AI
The uploaded PDF or image is parsed by multimodal vision extraction (Gemini), returning structured fields against a strict schema. Instructed to return null rather than guess. The only probabilistic step, and its output is presented to you in an inspection modal for review before anything is computed.
2
Check the document reconciles Deterministic
Base + taxes + fees must equal the stated total, or the case blocks. Components are never adjusted to force a match.
3
Resolve the policy version Deterministic
Filtered by carrier, market, fare family and the date in force. Dual-reading validation resolves under both booking date and cancellation date; open-ended observed windows stop at the evidence horizon.
4
Apply statute, then the fare rule Deterministic
Statutory taxes refund by law on every Indian carrier. The penalty nets into the base fare exactly once. YQ/YR are not taxes and are handled separately.
5
Gate the confidence Deterministic
Boolean gates, never a score. Rollup is restricted to material lines. A published policy table can never reach HIGH — only statute or a filed fare rule can.
6
Answer, or abstain Refuses
Reissued, partly flown, multi-passenger, foreign currency — no figure at all. A corporate fare abstains on the fare line only, because the governing agreement is a document the client can supply; its statutory taxes still compute, and the total is labelled PARTIAL. Abstention is a result, not a failure.

Why abstention is the point

A system that is usually right is worth less than one that reliably says "I cannot establish this." The failure mode of the alternative is a confident wrong number sitting in a financial record.

The honest ceiling

Only 2 of 5 carriers publish an effective date for their own fee tables, so most rules are capped at LOW — we know a rule was visible on a capture date, not when it started.

A negotiated fare is governed by the client's own agreement rather than the published retail table, so the engine gives a statutory PARTIAL total and names the document it would need, rather than applying the wrong table.
